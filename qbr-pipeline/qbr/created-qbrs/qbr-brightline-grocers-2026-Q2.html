1  /  Feature adoption

Notification usage is the standout this quarter. showNotification fired 12,722 times, up from 6,167, more than doubling quarter on quarter. setDiscount stayed steady, 3,507 uses against 3,225 prior, a modest 8.7% rise.

Two entitled capabilities sit unused: acceptCoupon and rejectCoupon have never fired despite being included on the plan. Both are available capability today, not a gap to close urgently, but worth a conversation if coupon-based promotions are on the roadmap.

2  /  Worth discussing

Two reliability movements stand out this quarter, both server error rate increases on customer-facing applications. Brightline Grocers Web ran at 1.9% error rate against a usual 0.5%, and Brightline Grocers Mobile ran at 1.1% against a usual 0.3%. Both are customer-visible and both touch revenue-affecting traffic.

The data shows these moved, not why. Worth asking whether the team saw anything on their side around early and mid June that lines up with either spike, and whether either is still elevated.

A handful of other movements were reviewed and set aside, mostly effect-firing counts on setDiscount and showNotification, because they carried lower commercial weight or thin volume relative to the two error rate jumps above.
