1  /  Feature adoption

Acme's engine usage grew across the board this quarter. showNotification led volume at 15,020 uses, up from 14,078. setDiscount followed at 13,261, up from 12,486, and addLoyaltyPoints rose to 5,911 from 5,578. The standout is rejectCoupon, which grew from 3,015 to 4,328 uses, a swing of roughly 44%, moving it from occasional use into a regular part of the flow.

acceptCoupon is the one feature moving the other way, down from 4,909 to 4,183 uses, about a 15% decline. All five capabilities in this review are entitled and active; there is no unused entitlement to flag this quarter. Worth watching whether acceptCoupon's dip and rejectCoupon's rise are two sides of the same shift in how shoppers are responding at checkout.

2  /  Worth discussing

The one movement worth raising this quarter is the redemption rate on Winback Reactivation, a paid feature. It landed at 48.2% for the week of 6 April, against a usual rate of 61.5%, a drop of about 21.6%. Because Winback sits behind a paid entitlement and the movement runs counter to where the account wants it, it is worth a direct conversation rather than a passing mention.

Acme has already explained the cause themselves. On the 6 May check-in, Priya Raman and Tomas Lindqvist told the account team: "We cut the winback audience down to ninety days in the first week of April, and honestly we expected the redemption rate to drop. That was the trade." [C1] The narrower audience is a deliberate choice on Acme's side, not a signal of campaign fatigue. The conversation worth having is whether the narrower audience is still producing the volume and quality of reactivation Acme wants, now that it has had a full quarter to run.

3  /  Since we last spoke

Two of the three actions from the last review are closed. Acme reviewed Winback Reactivation eligibility against margin ahead of the Q2 refresh and narrowed the eligibility window, a commitment now marked done. The account team's commitment to share Basket Boost segment performance so Acme could plan Q3 budget is also done.

One action remains open: agreeing a points expiry or redemption catalogue so the loyalty balance stops accruing unchecked. This sits with Acme, was raised again on a recent call, and is still unscheduled. Worth putting a date on it this quarter.
