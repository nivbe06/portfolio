Acme walked through their Q2 promotional calendar. Most of the call was about
the Winback Reactivation campaign, which they narrowed in early April.

Priya explained that the eligibility window was cut from 180 days to 90 days of
inactivity, and that the change was made without telling anyone outside her
team. The stated reason was margin: the 180-day cohort was converting, but on
discounts deep enough that the campaign was close to unprofitable.

> "We cut the winback audience down to ninety days in the first week of April,
> and honestly we expected the redemption rate to drop. That was the trade."
> - Priya Raman, 04:12

Tomas added that the creative was not refreshed alongside the audience change,
so the offer language still speaks to a lapsed shopper who has been gone six
months.

> "The creative still talks to someone who has been away half a year. We are
> now showing it to people who bought from us in March."
> - Tomas Lindqvist, 11:47

They intend to leave the narrower audience in place through Q3 and rewrite the
creative rather than widen it back out.
