Called at Acme's request after their finance team asked how the points balance
is accounted for.

Dana raised that the outstanding points balance has grown every quarter since
the Loyalty Tier Accelerator launched, and that nothing in the programme causes
points to be spent. There is an earn mechanic and no burn mechanic.

> "We built a very good way to give points away and we never built the reason
> to spend them. Finance is now carrying that as a liability and it grows every
> quarter."
> - Dana Osei, 07:31

Priya confirmed there is no redemption catalogue and no expiry policy, and that
adding either is on their roadmap but unscheduled.

Action agreed on the call: the account team to show the issued-against-burned
trend at the next QBR so Dana can take it to her CFO.
