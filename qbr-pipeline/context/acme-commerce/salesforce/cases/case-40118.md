Acme raised that they were seeing a higher volume of coupon rejections in early
April and asked whether something had changed on the platform.

Investigated and closed as expected behaviour. The rejections are the narrowed
Winback eligibility rule doing its job: shoppers outside the new 90-day window
present a code and are correctly refused. No platform defect. Tomas accepted the
explanation on 2026-05-12.
